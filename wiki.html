Blossom Vale Wiki

Blossom Vale is a cozy, peaceful Minecraft server: a pastel village hub, a survival world (the SMP) and a set of friendly mini-games, all in one network with one level, one set of friends and one currency.

Joining Blossom Vale

Jump toHow to joinResource packGood to knowQuestions

What you can do here

  • Live in the Blossom Vale SMP: survival with friends, claims that protect your builds, homes, quests, shops and no PvP.
  • Play mini-games from the village: Build Battle, Cozy Islands, SkyWars, BedWars and Ice Boat Racing.
  • Hang out in the village hub: emotes, photo spots, festivals, cosmetics and a wardrobe.

When you join you arrive in the village hub. Walk to the Games Plaza, or open the Game Selector, to pick where to go.

Required: accept the resource pack. The first time you join, the server asks you to download the Blossom Vale resource pack. You must accept it: if you decline, you are disconnected. The pack gives you the Blossom Vale menus, rank badges, small-caps text, the level bar at the top of the screen and the animated emotes. Bedrock players get the same menus as chests and chat prompts, and probably will not see the animated emotes.

Good to know

  • Blossom Vale is peaceful: no griefing and no PvP on the SMP. Survival-style games (Cozy Islands, SkyWars, BedWars) still have normal hunger, so bring food.
  • Your village level, Petals, friends, tags and cosmetics follow you to every server of the network.
  • Type /commands (or /help) in game for a help window.
  • Join the community on Discord: https://discord.gg/YCUe6CXVaB. In game, type /discord, or click the Discord banner at the top of the Village Menu.

Questions people ask

Do I need the resource pack?

Yes. The server asks for it when you first join, and you are disconnected if you decline. It gives you the Blossom Vale menus, badges, level bar, small-caps text and animated emotes.

Can I play on Bedrock?

Yes, on port 19132. Bedrock players get chest-style menus and chat prompts instead of the custom windows, and probably will not see the animated emotes.

Is the SMP whitelisted?

No, it is open to everyone.

Can I lose my items when I die?

Keep inventory is on by default. Turn it off or on with /keepinv.

Can other players grief my builds?

Claim your builds with the Claim Lantern. Staff can also undo damage with the block log.

How do I invite a friend to a game?

Make a private room in the game's menu, then invite from your friends list. They get a [Join] button.

Why did I get no XP from a game?

Practice and private-room rounds give no XP, and rewarded rounds have a daily limit.

How do I report someone?

Use /report <player> or message a staff member.

Mini-games

Five mini-games run on the games server. Open the Game Selector in the village hub, pick a game, then pick a format. Each menu shows how many players are waiting. Leaving or finishing any game sends you back to the hub, and /leave steps out at any time.

GamePlayersWhat you do
Build Battle2 to 8Build a theme in a short time, then vote on each other's builds
Cozy IslandsJust you (visitors welcome)Grow your own floating island by finishing goals
SkyWars2 to 16Loot floating islands, be the last one standing
BedWars2 to 16Guard your bed, break the others, spend what you collect
Ice Boat Racing2 to 8Race boats on icy tracks, 12 maps

All games give village XP. Rewarded rounds have a daily limit. Practice and private-room rounds give no XP.

Build Battle

Everyone builds on their own plot with a shared theme and a timer (about 6 minutes in a solo round), then players vote on the builds.

  • Formats: solo, or teams of 2, 3 or 4.
  • Private rooms: invite friends. The host sets things like the build time.
  • Practice is available from the waiting lobby.

Cozy Islands

A skyblock-style game. Every player owns a small floating island that is kept for good.

  • First time: pick a look (meadow, snowy or cherry). You start with a tree, a small pond and a starter chest.
  • Goals: finish the goals of a level (break, place, craft, harvest, fish, breed, visit, trust friends and more) to level up your island. A bigger level gives more land and a bigger build area. Goals give village XP and useful items.
  • Goal map: your goals are shown as connected nodes, and the current goal sits at the top of the screen with a bar.
  • Friends: anyone can visit an open island but only look. Trust a friend to let them build with you. You can close your island to visitors.
  • Start over: wipes the island and its goals (trusted friends are kept). There is a cooldown of 6 hours.
  • Rules of the world: hunger works as normal (the starter chest has bread, and you can farm or fish for more), but there is no fall damage and no mobs or fire spread. Falling into the void puts you back on your island.
  • Commands: /island takes you to your island.

SkyWars

Each team starts in a glass cage on its own island in the sky. When the cages open, loot the chests, build bridges and fight. The void is a way to lose. Last one standing wins.

  • Formats: Solo (2 to 8 players), Duos (teams of 2, needs 4 or more), Squads (4 teams of 4, needs 8 or more), private room and practice.
  • Maps: eight cozy themes (mushroom grove, blossom shrine, flower grove, autumn village, snowy village, tropical and more). Duos and squads play on bigger maps.
  • Round flow: 10 seconds in the cages, a short no-fighting start, hunger on (food is in the chests), chests refill once partway through, and late in the round the sky closes in towards the middle island.
  • Private room: invite friends, pick bump mode (hits only push, the void is the only way out) or real fights, pick the map, and add practice dummies.

BedWars

Every team has an island with a bed. While your bed is there you come back 5 seconds after a fall. When it is gone, the next fall is your last. Last team standing wins. Fights are real.

  • Formats: Solo (a bed each), Duos, Squads (4 teams of 4), private room and practice.
  • Resources: iron and gold appear on your island, diamonds on the small outer islands and the middle tower, and emeralds on the middle island.
  • Item shop (villager on your island): blocks, weapons, armour, tools and extras (including steak, since hunger is on), paid in iron, gold and emeralds.
  • Team upgrades (second villager, paid in diamonds): sharper swords, tougher armour, quick hands, an iron forge, a healing pool near your bed and a trap.
  • Rules: only blocks that players placed can be broken. You cannot break your own bed. Whoever sends you off an island with your bed gone takes what you carried.
  • Round clock: diamonds speed up at 6:00, emeralds at 8:00, every bed breaks at 12:00 (13:00 in squads), then the sky closes in.
  • Maps: eight cozy themes, including candy land and a lantern festival.

Ice Boat Racing

Intense but cozy racing on 12 icy maps, each with its own idea: a ski jump, rising pillars, a snowdrift slalom, a dark cave, open lagoons, a town circuit and more.

  • Join: everyone in the village is entered in the next race automatically (up to 8). Vote for the map and the mode on the vote screen. Use the dye in your hotbar to sit out.
  • Modes: Classic, Grand Prix (four maps with points), Elimination (last racer drops each lap), Cozy Kart (snowflake item boxes) and Team Relay.
  • Challenges: race one friend (best of 1 or 3), race a saved ghost, or make a private room.
  • Penguin bots: add them on request for practice. They are clearly marked as bots and never count for wins or records.
  • Medals and times: each map has bronze, silver, gold and diamond times, best times and a weekly Map of the Week.
  • Extras: boat cosmetics (wood, trail, horn, finish firework), a race rank (Snowflake up to Blizzard) and replays of recent races. Rewards are village XP only.

The Blossom Vale SMP

Jump toGetting aroundClaimsTeams and friendsQuestsEconomy

The SMP is the heart of Blossom Vale: an open, peaceful survival world for everyone. Players join from the big gateway in the village hub, or with /smp.

  • Open to all: there is no whitelist.
  • No PvP, easy difficulty, and a world border of 20,000 blocks across.
  • Keep inventory is on by default. Each player can switch it with /keepinv (you are told about this on your first join).
  • Spawn is protected: only staff can build within 48 blocks of the Overworld spawn.
  • Nether and End: unlock by portal. The End also needs the Ender Dragon to be defeated first.

First join

New players get a welcome title, a short guide, a Claim Lantern in their bag and buttons to open the SMP menu or explore straight away.

Claims

Protect your builds with the Claim Lantern: hold it where you want your claim, right-click, look at the preview and confirm. Nothing is placed in your build.

  • A claim is a 21 x 21 square. You can own up to 3 claims, and ranks add more.
  • The /claims window shows borders, lets you make a claim bigger (paid upgrades up to 101 x 101, price shown before you buy), trust friends, rename or give a claim up.
  • You earn free growth credits by playing (one per 10 hours), plus bonuses at village levels 10, 20 and 30.
  • Claims cannot be made near spawn or close to the centre of the End, and each needs a small gap from other players' claims. Trusted friends and teammates can claim right next to yours.
  • Staff can undo damage in claimed or unclaimed areas using the block log.

Teams and friends

  • /team and /tc (team chat): teams of up to 8 (more if the team leader has a rank) can build on each other's claims.
  • /trade <player>: a safe two-player trade window. Both players press Ready, then there is a 3 second countdown. Nothing moves unless both have room.
  • /visit and /friend: visit public lands and join friends.
  • Sleep voting: the night skips when half of the awake Overworld players are sleeping.

Quests

Open the Wanderer's Journal with /quests.

  • Daily quests (small Petal rewards) and weekly quests (bigger rewards).
  • Big one-time quests worth 30 to 50 Petals, some of which unlock village tags (Dragon Slayer, Wither Breaker, Warden Bane and more).
  • Storylines, seasonal quests and a weekly "Together" goal for the whole server.
  • A daily streak and bonuses for finishing everything. You get one quest swap a day.

Economy

  • Petals are the village currency. They come from levels, quests, weekly boards and events, and are not easy to get. Send some to a friend with /petals pay <player> <amount> (up to 50 a day, and you need 2 hours in the village first).
  • Coins are money for shops and land upgrades. Pay with /pay, and see the richest with /baltop.
  • Player shops: players set their own prices.
  • Rewards for Petals and levels are mostly badges, tags and cosmetics.

Village features

Village levels

One level for the whole network, from 1 to 30 (then stars). Check it with /level.

  • Earn XP by doing anything: chatting, being active, a daily login, emotes with friends, hub activities, mini-games, and on the SMP mining, farming, fighting, fishing, breeding, crafting, building on your claim, exploring and quests. A mix of activities is fastest.
  • Double XP on Saturdays and Sundays, and double XP during a new player's first week.
  • Pace: about level 10 in 2 to 3 hours of play, level 20 in about 8 and level 30 in about 16.
  • Rewards: 1 Petal per level, bonus Petals at levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30, and village tags at levels 10, 20 and 30.
  • SMP perks: an extra home at levels 5, 15 and 25, and a free claim growth at levels 10, 20 and 30.
  • Weekly top three (/level top, at least 100 XP) win Petals the following week. Reaching level 10, 20 or 30 is announced to everyone.
  • Your level shows as a pink bar at the top of the screen and on the sidebar.

Emotes

Use /emote to open a picker, or type one directly: wave, clap, bow, dance, twirl, hop, shy, nod, shake, sit, blow a kiss, think, laugh, stretch and salute. They are animated and ease in and out, and walking away stops them. They need the resource pack.

Profile, friends and cosmetics

  • /profile shows a card with your level, lands, homes and team. Hovering a name in chat shows rank, tag, level and playtime, and clicking opens the card.
  • /friend manages your friends list. Friends can invite you to private game rooms.
  • The wardrobe holds your cosmetics. Tags come in 21 pastel looks, and badges and tags show next to your name.
  • Time ranks are free and automatic: neighbour, villager and elder.
  • Neighbour of the week (/neighbour) is a weekly nomination with a 100 Petal prize.
  • Handy extras: /hat, /ptime, /pweather, /seen, /trash.

Rules and community

Blossom Vale is built to be kind and relaxed. Everyone is expected to follow these rules.

  1. Griefing and stealing. Respect other players' builds and belongings. Griefing and stealing are prohibited unless explicitly allowed in a gamemode.
  2. Hacking and exploiting. Hacked clients, x-ray, duping and abusing glitches are prohibited. Report exploits instead of using them.
  3. PvP and fair play. PvP is only allowed where the gamemode permits it or when players agree. No unfair teaming, boosting, or spawn camping where prohibited.
  4. Scamming and trading. Tricking players out of items or currency is not allowed. Real-money trading outside approved server systems is prohibited.
  5. Building and community areas. Respect land claims, public areas and other players' creations. Do not build to intentionally obstruct or annoy others.
  6. Farms and server performance. Lag machines, excessive entities, and farms that significantly affect server performance are not permitted.
  7. Alternate accounts. Using alternate accounts to gain extra rewards, manipulate rankings, or bypass punishments is prohibited.
  8. Gamemode-specific rules. Each gamemode may have different rules for PvP, building and progression. Follow the rules of whichever gamemode you are playing.

Reporting a problem

Use /report <player> in game. The report includes a short summary of what the server saw that player do in the last 30 minutes, and goes to the staff team. For anything private, message a staff member directly.

Punishments

Staff can warn, mute, kick and ban. Short mutes and bans are used for small things; permanent bans are only for serious or repeated problems.
