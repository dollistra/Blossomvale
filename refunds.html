Refund policy

Our refund promise, in plain words.

Last updated 6 November 2026

Terms of servicePrivacy policyRefund policy

Ranks are digital items that are delivered straight away, so once your rank has arrived, it is final and we do not give refunds for a change of mind. We always fix anything that goes wrong, and we will refund you if we cannot.

Why ranks are final once delivered

When you buy, you are asked to agree that your rank is delivered straight away, and that this means you give up the 14-day right to cancel that normally applies to online purchases. Ranks work the moment they arrive, so we cannot offer a trial period. Please be sure before you buy, and ask us any questions first on Discord or at hello@blossomvale.world.

When we will fix it or refund you

  • Your rank did not arrive. It normally arrives within a few minutes. Rejoin the server first. If it is still missing after 15 minutes, contact us and we will fix it or refund you.
  • You were charged twice, or the amount was wrong.
  • It went to the wrong account. Tell us quickly and we will move it or refund you.
  • Something does not work as we described. If a perk you bought is broken or missing, we will fix it, replace it or refund you.
  • You bought it by accident, for example the wrong rank, the wrong Minecraft name, or you paid with the wrong card or payment account. Contact us within 48 hours, before you have used the rank (claimed the monthly kit, picked seasonal items, or set a nickname or join message), and we will refund you so you can buy again the right way. We may ask a few questions to check.
  • The payment was not authorised by the person who owns the card or account. This includes a child who bought a rank without a parent's permission, or someone who used another person's card. The card or account owner (or a parent or guardian) can contact us within 14 days of the purchase, with the Minecraft name and the email used at checkout, and we will refund it. We may ask a few questions to check. Please also tell your bank if your card was used without your permission.

How to ask

Email hello@blossomvale.world or open a ticket in our Discord. Please give us your Minecraft name and the email you used at checkout. We aim to reply within 2 days. Refunds go back to the card or PayPal account you paid with, and can take up to 14 business days to show up, depending on your bank.

What happens to your rank

When we refund a rank, the rank and everything that came only with it is removed: rank tags and colours, trails, nickname, join message, claim colour and message, seasonal picks and the monthly kit. Petals and crate keys we already gave you, and anything you bought with them, are yours to keep, unless we refunded you because of a payment problem, in which case we may take them back.

Upgrades

The same rules apply to upgrades. If we refund an upgrade, you go back to the rank you had before and get the difference back.

Gifts

If you buy a rank for a friend, any refund goes to the person who paid, and the rank is removed from your friend.

Rule-breaking

If an account is banned for breaking the village rules, its rank is not refunded.

Chargebacks

Please ask us before asking your bank to reverse a payment, because we are quicker and happy to help. A payment reversed through the bank removes the rank, and we may stop selling to that account.

Your legal rights

This policy sits alongside your rights under the law where you live, such as your rights if digital content is faulty or not as described, and does not take them away. It also sits alongside the Terms of service.
