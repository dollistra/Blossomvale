Refund policy

Our refund promise, in plain words.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Terms of servicePrivacy policyRefund policy

Ranks, Petals and crate keys are digital items delivered to the Minecraft account entered at checkout. We will look into missing or incorrect purchases, duplicate charges, unauthorised payments, and anything faulty or not as described. If you change your mind, your cancellation and refund rights depend on applicable consumer law and the terms shown at checkout. This policy does not limit your legal rights.

Digital purchases

Our shop is operated through Tebex. Please check the item, amount, Minecraft name and any gift recipient carefully before paying. Digital items may be delivered as soon as payment is confirmed. Your statutory rights still apply. Where immediate digital delivery affects a cancellation right, the required consent and acknowledgement are handled at checkout.

When we will fix it or refund you

  • Your purchase did not arrive. Rejoin the server first. If it is still missing after 15 minutes, contact us and we will check the order and put it right, including a refund where required.
  • You were charged twice, or the amount was wrong.
  • It went to the wrong account. Contact us promptly. We will check whether it can be corrected safely and discuss the options available under your legal rights.
  • Something does not work as described. We will investigate and, where appropriate, fix it, replace it or refund you.
  • You bought it by accident, for example the wrong item or Minecraft name. Contact us as soon as possible—ideally within 48 hours and before using the digital item—so we can try to correct the order quickly. The 48-hour timeframe is a support guideline, not a deadline for exercising any legal cancellation or refund right. We may ask for details to confirm the order.
  • The payment was not authorised. The card or payment-account owner, or a parent or guardian for a child, should contact us as soon as possible with the order details. We may ask for information needed to investigate. Please also contact your bank if your payment details were used without permission.

How to ask

Email hello@blossomvale.world or open a ticket in our Discord. Please give us your Minecraft name, order number and the email used at checkout. We aim to reply within 2 days. Approved refunds are processed through Tebex to the original payment method; timing depends on the payment provider.

What happens to your rank

When a rank purchase is refunded, we will remove the refunded rank's benefits from the Minecraft account and, where linked and supported by the store integration, its matching Discord supporter role. If the refunded purchase was an upgrade, we will restore the lower rank that the player had already paid for where the account history allows us to verify it. For refunded Petals or crate keys, we may remove the unspent amount or keys delivered by that order; items already used may need to be handled case by case.

Upgrades

Rank upgrades are handled by purchasing the higher rank; eligible previous rank purchases are taken into account by Tebex at checkout. If an upgrade is refunded, we will review the order history and restore any lower rank that remains paid for.

Gifts

If you gift a digital item, contact us using the order details. Any approved refund goes to the payer through Tebex, and we will remove the refunded item or rank benefit from the recipient where possible.

Rule-breaking

If an account is banned for breaking the village rules, its rank is not refunded.

Chargebacks

Please contact us first if you think something went wrong, so we can investigate promptly. A payment reversal may lead to removal of the associated digital items and rank benefits, including linked Discord roles, and may restrict future purchases while the dispute is reviewed.

Your legal rights

This policy sits alongside your rights under the law where you live, such as your rights if digital content is faulty or not as described, and does not take them away. It also sits alongside the Terms of service.
