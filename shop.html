    Questions

    How do I buy a rank?

    Ranks and Petals will be sold through our store when the village opens. You will pick a rank, type your Minecraft username and pay through a secure payment provider, so we never see your card details. Your rank then arrives on its own.

    How long does my rank last?

    Ranks are a one-time purchase with no monthly fee, and they last for as long as Blossom Vale is running. We may add more perks over time, and we will try to give them to everyone who already has the rank.

    Can I upgrade later?

    Yes, you only pay the difference between your rank and the next one. See the upgrade table above.

    Do ranks give an advantage in games?

    No. Ranks are cosmetics plus small conveniences, such as extra homes and more /rtp uses. They never change how BedWars, SkyWars, Build Battle or Ice Boat Racing are played or who wins.

    What are Petals, and are they random?

    Petals are our cosmetic currency. You can earn them by playing or buy bundles, and they only buy looks like tags, colours, trails, hats and pets. Nothing is random: with a key you choose the exact piece you want.

    Does it work on Bedrock?

    Ranks, tags and chat colours should work for everyone. Some visual extras, like animated emotes and custom hats and wings, may not show on Bedrock. We are still confirming this.

    What about refunds?

    Ranks are delivered straight away, so they are final once delivered and we do not refund a change of mind. But if something goes wrong we will fix it or refund you: it did not arrive, you were charged twice, it went to the wrong account, you bought it by accident or paid with the wrong card (tell us within 48 hours, before using it), or someone paid without the card owner's permission, such as a child without a parent's OK (tell us within 14 days). See our Refund policy.

    I am under 18. Can I buy things?

    Please ask a parent or guardian first. Ranks and Petals are never required to enjoy Blossom Vale.
