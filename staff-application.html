Join the Blossom Vale Team

Would you like to help make Blossom Vale a friendly place to play? Tell us a little about yourself and the kind of help you’d like to offer. We’ll get back to you on Discord if there’s a suitable opening.

Staff Application

Please answer in your own words. A few questions will change depending on the role you choose. You don’t need previous staff experience to apply.

Enable Developer Mode in Discord, then right-click your profile and choose Copy User ID. Please send the ID, not your username.

Helper questions

Please explain your reasoning and the steps you’d take, not just the final action. You don’t need to know any specific server commands, plugins, or tools.

Content creator questions

Choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP screenshot (up to 10 MB) showing your channel name or handle and audience size. Hide private details. We’ll store the screenshot with your application for reviewers.

Application answers and creator proof screenshots are stored in Blossom Vale’s Firebase project and can only be accessed by authorized reviewers. See our privacy policy.
