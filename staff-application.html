Join the Blossom Vale Team

Would you like to help make Blossom Vale a friendly place to play? Tell us a little about yourself and the kind of help you’d like to offer. We’ll get back to you on Discord if there’s a suitable opening.

Before you apply

Please read these three short lists first. Tap one to open it. They are the same for everyone, and they help us keep Blossom Vale a kind place.

Requirements 8 points
  • You are at least 14 years old.
  • You are in our Discord server and have played on the Minecraft server.
  • You have turned on two-factor authentication on your Discord account.
  • You have no active punishments and haven’t broken a rule in the last 30 days.
  • You can give about 3 to 5 hours a week, and you’ll tell us if you’ll be away.
  • You can chat clearly and kindly in English.
  • Helper: no experience needed. We’ll show you the rest.
  • Content Creator: your own channel that posts regularly, with a screenshot to show it’s yours.
What we expect from the team 8 points
  • Be kind and fair to everyone, including your friends.
  • Keep private things private: reports, staff chat, player details and screenshots.
  • Never ask for or accept items, ranks, money or favours for anything you do as staff.
  • Only use your permissions for the job, never for fun or to help yourself or friends.
  • Follow the rules yourself. Staff are held to them too.
  • If you’re not sure, ask a senior team member. It’s fine not to know.
  • New team members start with a short trial period.
  • Tell us if you’ll be away for more than two weeks. You can step down at any time.
Application guidelines 6 points
  • Write your answers yourself, in your own words. Applications written or rewritten with AI (like ChatGPT) will be declined straight away, and so will copied or shared answers.
  • Be honest. We would rather hear “I’m not sure, but I would ask” than a perfect-sounding answer.
  • Send one application only. Please don’t send another while yours is being looked at.
  • Use your real Discord User ID and Minecraft username, so we can check them.
  • Don’t message staff to ask about your application. We’ll contact you on Discord if there’s a suitable opening, and we may ask follow-up questions.
  • If you’re declined, you can apply again after 30 days.

Staff Application

Please answer in your own words. A few questions will change depending on the role you choose. You don’t need previous staff experience to apply, but please tell us about any experience you do have.

Enable Developer Mode in Discord, then right-click your profile and choose Copy User ID. Please send the ID, not your username.

Optional, but it helps. Add up to 3 screenshots (PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 10 MB each), such as a staff role list or a server you helped run. Hide private details like other people's names and messages.

Helper questions

Be specific: say what you would actually do and what you would say, and why. Short, general answers won’t be enough. The section about Blossom Vale checks how well you know the server, so name the real commands and places where you can. You can look around our website, wiki and Discord first.

About you 2 questions
Knowing Blossom Vale 4 questions
In-game scenarios 2 questions
Discord questions 4 questions

Content creator questions

Choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP screenshot (up to 10 MB) showing your channel name or handle and audience size. Hide private details. It will be sent with your application to the Blossom Vale review team in Discord.

Your application is delivered to the private Blossom Vale staff review channel in Discord. Access depends on that channel’s permissions. See our privacy policy.
