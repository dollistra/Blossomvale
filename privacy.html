Privacy policy

Your information, in plain words.

Last updated 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information Blossom Vale collects about you, why, and what your choices are. We try to collect only what we need to run a friendly, safe server.

The short version: we collect what the game needs to work (your Minecraft name, ID and game progress), some safety records (so staff can deal with griefing and rule-breaking), and purchase records. We do not sell your information, show ads, or use cookies or tracking tools on this website.

1. Who we are

Blossom Vale (“we”, “us”) decides how and why your information is used on our Minecraft server network, this website and our shop. You can contact us at hello@blossomvale.world.

3. This website

You do not need an account to use this website, and it does not set cookies, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools. To work, the pages ask a few other services for information, and those services can see your IP address when they do:

  • Our website host serves the pages and keeps ordinary access records.
  • Google Firebase supplies the live server status shown on the home page.
  • mcstatus.io tells the home page whether the server is online and how many players are on.
  • mc-heads.net draws your skin picture, but only if you type your Minecraft name into a preview on the Shop page. Your name is sent to them to fetch the picture, and nothing else is stored by us.

Links to Discord, Tebex and other sites take you away from ours, and their own privacy policies apply there. If you link your Discord account when you buy, we will see your Discord name so that we can give you your supporter role.

4. Who we share it with

  • Tebex, who take your payment and handle our store. They use your payment details under their own privacy policy. We may also share details of a payment with them, or with a bank or payment provider, if there is a dispute, a refund or suspected fraud.
  • Our staff, who see only what they need to do their job.
  • The services we use to run the server and website, including hosting and the places we keep backups.
  • The authorities, if the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Some of the services above (such as Tebex and Google) may handle information outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on approved safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Game data: for as long as you play with us, and for a limited time afterwards in case you come back. We delete it sooner if you ask.
  • Server logs and backups: for a limited time, then they are deleted.
  • Moderation records: for as long as we need them to enforce a ban or look at an appeal.
  • Purchase records: for as long as the law requires us to keep accounting records, which is usually several years.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information (some records, like purchase records, we may have to keep);
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to a use that relies on our legitimate interest;
  • give you your information in a portable form, or take back your consent where we rely on it.

Email hello@blossomvale.world from the address you used with us, or tell us in Discord, with your Minecraft name. We will reply within one month. If you are not happy with how we handled something, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

7. Children

Many of our players are young, so we keep to what the game needs. Players under 13 may only play with a parent or guardian's permission. We do not show ads, we do not sell information, and we do not build profiles of players to target them. Chat is filtered and moderated, and we ask everyone not to share personal information such as their real name, address, school or phone number. A parent or guardian can email us to see, correct or delete their child's information.

8. Keeping it safe

We protect our systems with passwords, permissions and backups, and only staff who need access can reach your information. No system is perfectly secure. If a problem puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the authorities where the law says we must.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add a feature that uses information in a new way. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will post a notice about important changes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: hello@blossomvale.world.
